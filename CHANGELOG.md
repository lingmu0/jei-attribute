# JEI Attribute 1.0.0 — Minecraft 26.1.2 NeoForge

- Ported the attribute search and detail viewer to Minecraft 26.1.2 and NeoForge.
- Updated integration for JEI 29 and optional Curios 15.
- Updated Chinese and English descriptions for all 35 vanilla attributes in 26.1.2.
- Retained translated initial icons, Recipes and Uses lookups, cycling source items, advanced-only attribute IDs, and the gray player calculation with detailed modifier tooltips.

Requires Java 25, NeoForge 26.1.2.107 or newer in the 26.1.2 series, and JEI 29.43.0.106 or newer in the JEI 29 series. Curios 15 is optional.
